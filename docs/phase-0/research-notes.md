# Phase 0 Research Notes and Source Register

**Research date:** 5 November 2026. Sources must be rechecked before launch because laws, products, and URLs change.

## Cultural and language context

- Mithila is generally described as the cultural region of north Bihar and adjoining southeastern Nepal; Maithili is the region's Indo-Aryan language and is constitutionally recognized in India. Treat geography and identity as culturally meaningful but not as a single uniform matrimonial rule set.
- Public scholarship and cultural histories describe Panji-Prabandh as genealogical record keeping associated especially with Maithil Brahmin communities and used in marriage-lineage decisions. Public accounts vary on origin dates, coverage, terminology, and authority.
- Public accounts describe Panjikars as genealogists/record keepers and Saurath Sabha as a traditional matrimonial assembly in Madhubani. These claims require interviews with current knowledgeable practitioners before product claims or workflows are finalized.

## Market scan

- **Shaadi.com:** broad scale, large discovery network, privacy controls, verification signals, and assisted/premium services. Opportunity: culturally precise Maithil terminology, consented family delegation, and transparent lineage uncertainty.
- **BharatMatrimony / CommunityMatrimony:** strong regional and community segmentation, assisted matchmaking, and verification offerings. Opportunity: a single Maithil-first experience rather than a portal among hundreds, with expert-reviewed cultural content.
- **Jeevansathi:** strong North/West India presence, community search, and profile verification. Opportunity: explainable recommendations, better lineage provenance, and deeper owner/delegate consent.
- **Niche/community groups and offline networks:** trust and local knowledge, but inconsistent safety, searchability, and availability across diaspora. Opportunity: digitize coordination without claiming to replace local authority.

## Evidence limitations

Search snippets and platform marketing pages are directional, not independent evidence. Product claims must cite primary documents or reviewed interviews. Do not publish exact Gotra/Mool lists, marriage prohibitions, certificate names, or genealogy records from an unverified web source.

## Interview protocol

Ask practitioners what terms mean in their community, which fields are required or private, how Panji records are accessed, who may review them, what uncertainty looks like, how families handle disagreement, and which digital workflows would be harmful. Record consent, anonymize notes, and have at least one Maithili-language reviewer check translations and public copy.
